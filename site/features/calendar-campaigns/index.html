A Calendar campaign creates a Google Calendar event on your sender’s calendar and adds your contacts as attendees. Google delivers the invitation, and each Yes, No, or Maybe becomes a pipeline signal. An opted-in campaign registers Yes and Maybe responses into your linked webinar.
