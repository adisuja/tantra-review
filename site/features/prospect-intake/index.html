    Where Prospect Intake Fits

    Any moment a new prospect appears can start an omnichannel sequence.

    Form fills and webinar sign-ups

    Your form or automation tool sends each new sign-up to a follow-up campaign by webhook. The sequence opens with email and continues on LinkedIn and WhatsApp.

    CRM stage changes

    When a deal is lost or an account goes quiet, your CRM automation sends the contact to a re-engagement campaign without an export.

    Accounts researched by your AI assistant

    Ask your assistant to find accounts that match your criteria, qualify them and add the right people to the right omnichannel campaign.
