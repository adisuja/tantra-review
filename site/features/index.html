01 · Omnichannel sequences

Coordinated outreach across three channels

Build one sequence of ordered steps across Gmail, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, with a delay before each step and a stored template behind every message. A sequence can run on social channels alone. When a reply is detected, the sequence stops for that contact. AI can personalize email content from the prospect's website, with the stored template as the fallback; social steps use templates, variables and spintax.

Three channels, one sequence

Each channel sends from its own connected account. If a contact has no identity for a channel, that step is skipped rather than failing the sequence, and a LinkedIn message can wait until the connection request is accepted.

Setup and limits

Pro includes up to 5 LinkedIn or WhatsApp accounts and Scale up to 15, and you can add more at $20 per additional account per month. Google Workspace is needed only for Calendar campaigns and Gmail steps.

Template fallback

If website research or AI personalization cannot complete, Tantra sends the approved stored template instead.

Validation before enrollment

Syntax, MX and role-address checks run before a contact enters a sequence. Questionable addresses are quarantined rather than sent.

Suppression on every send

One-click unsubscribe headers on every message, and a suppression list checked before each send.

    No open tracking

    Tantra does not add open-tracking pixels. Replies, signed-link clicks, bounces and unsubscribes are the email signals it reports. With click tracking on, each new send uses your current verified tracking domain.

    04 · Calendar outreach

    Calendar invitations as a campaign type

    Tantra creates a Google Calendar event and adds recipients as attendees, so Google delivers the invitation and it appears on their calendar. Yes, No and Maybe responses are the engagement signal. Link Zoom or WebinarGeek to attribute registrations and attendance, and refresh that data on request.

    Sample data

    Campaign NameStatusWebinarYesMaybeNoWaitingSent

    Distributed across senders

    Large lists are spread across your verified Calendar senders in scheduled batches, and recoverable failures are retried automatically.

    RSVP webhooks

    Send Yes, No and Maybe responses to any HTTPS endpoint. Delivery is at least once, and every event carries an ID for deduplication.

    Webinar registration

    An opted-in Calendar campaign can register Yes and Maybe responses for a linked Zoom or WebinarGeek webinar. Registrant and attendance data refresh on request.

    08 · Integrations

    Campaign events in the rest of your stack

    Send campaign events to any HTTPS endpoint, automate Tantra through the REST API, or operate it from an AI client over MCP. n8n, Zapier and Make connect through webhooks.

    Webhook events for every channel

    Calendar RSVPs, email replies, clicks, bounces and unsubscribes, LinkedIn acceptances and replies, and WhatsApp replies. Delivery is at least once, with an event ID for deduplication.

    REST API with account-level keys

    Revocable keys with optional expiry. The full key is shown once, and each key carries account-level access.

    n8n, Zapier, and Make

    Route any webhook event through your automation tool to update a CRM record, alert Slack, enrich a contact, append a row or call your own service. These connect through webhooks rather than native apps.

    Google Workspace, Zoom, and WebinarGeek

    Verify Workspace senders for Gmail and Calendar. Zoom and WebinarGeek data refresh on request; an opted-in Calendar campaign can register positive RSVPs, and sequences provide attribution.
