How Personalization Works

One Model Call per Email

The model reads the prospect’s website, then writes the subject and body in a single call. Your provider bills you directly. Tantra adds no markup and sells no credits.

Your Template Is the Fallback

A webmail address with no company website, an unreadable site, or a provider error never blocks a step. The stored template sends instead.
