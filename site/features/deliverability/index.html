Five Safeguards on Every Send

Every email passes the same five controls, whichever campaign sends it.

Applied to every send0 of 5 safeguards
  1. 1Do-not-contact checkSuppressed and unsubscribed contacts are skipped
  2. 2Mailbox capsDaily and hourly limits for each mailbox
  3. 3Sending windowSends in the recipient’s timezone
  4. 4Bounce suppressionA hard bounce ends the sequence and suppresses the address
  5. 5One-click unsubscribeList-Unsubscribe header and a footer link

Sending Discipline, Built In

Checks at Enrollment and at Every Send

Suppressed and unsubscribed contacts are never enrolled, and a contact who opts out mid-campaign is removed from the remaining steps. Invalid addresses are quarantined for review. Outside the sending window or over a cap, the email waits.

Warmup Between Your Own Mailboxes

Two or more of your connected mailboxes exchange warmup mail. Each starts at 5 a day and adds 5 every 3 days toward the target you set, 100 a day by default.
