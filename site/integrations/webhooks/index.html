Integrations · Build and automate

Send engagement events out and bring prospects in.

Subscribe an HTTPS endpoint to the RSVP, email, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp events your downstream systems need, with a delivery history for every attempt. In the other direction, each campaign can receive prospects from your forms, CRM, Clay, Zapier, n8n or Make, and they enter its omnichannel sequence automatically.

AvailableDeveloper
Create a webhookRead setup guide

Requires an HTTPS endpoint that can receive POST requests.

What you get

Prospects in by webhook

Each campaign can receive prospects from a webhook, so a form, CRM, Clay, Zapier, n8n or Make sends a prospect straight into the campaign's sequence. No CSV upload and no manual start.

Independent endpoints

Register multiple endpoints, each with its own event subscriptions.

Selectable events

Subscribe each endpoint to the events it needs: RSVPs, email replies, clicks, bounces, unsubscribes, LinkedIn acceptances and replies, and WhatsApp replies.

Test payloads and headers

Send a schema-discovery test payload, with headers that include the event type and a unique event ID.

Durable delivery

Delivery attempts and a delivery history are recorded for every event.

At-least-once delivery

Delivery is at least once; deduplicate downstream using the event ID.

Full endpoint controls

Enable, disable, preview, test, edit, or delete an endpoint at any time. Social events fire only for channels connected on your plan.

Supported actions and data

Tantra can do

  • POST RSVP, email engagement, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp events to your endpoint
  • Receive prospects from your forms, CRM, Clay, Zapier, n8n or Make and add them to a campaign
  • Retry delivery and keep a delivery history per attempt

Tantra receives

  • A 2xx response from your endpoint acknowledging delivery
  • Prospects added to a campaign from your forms, CRM or automation tool

Requirements and permissions

  • An HTTPS endpoint that can receive POST requests

  • A handler that responds quickly, within the documented timeout

Scope and limits

  • Delivery is at least once; deduplicate downstream using the event ID.
  • Endpoints must respond within the documented timeout.
  • Calendar webhooks emit RSVP transitions, not unchanged snapshots.
  • Social replies must belong to a sequence-linked conversation.
  • n8n, Zapier, and Make connect through webhooks in both directions; they are not native Tantra connectors.
  • Every prospect passes the same suppression, validation, sending-limit and sending-window checks, however it arrives.
