Integrations · Outreach channels

WhatsApp follow-up inside your outreach sequences.

Link your existing WhatsApp number by scanning a QR code, add plain-text message steps to a sequence, and handle replies in the same inbox as every other channel. No WhatsApp Business API is required.

AvailableIncluded in Pro and Scale
Activate or connect WhatsAppRead setup guide

Requires an active Pro, Scale, or Enterprise plan.

What you get

QR-based connection

Link a standard WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app account with a QR code, the same way you add a linked device.

Plain-text message steps

Add plain-text WhatsApp message steps to a sequence.

International phone numbers

Messages send to contact phone numbers in E.164 format with a country code.

Daily and weekly limits

Each account starts at 50 messages a day, and per-account daily and weekly limits pace WhatsApp activity.

Account management

Sync, reconnect, or remove a connected WhatsApp account at any time.

Unified inbox and webhooks

Replies land in the unified inbox, and sequence-linked replies can trigger a webhook.

How it works

  1. 1

    Open WhatsApp under Connectors

  2. 2

    Scan the QR code from Linked devices on your phone

  3. 3

    Allow the initial sync

  4. 4

    Add a WhatsApp message step to a sequence

  5. 5

    Select the account in the campaign's Senders step

Connect and manage WhatsApp→Plans and pricing→

Supported actions and data

Tantra can do

  • Send plain-text WhatsApp messages to contact phone numbers
  • Add WhatsApp steps alongside email and LinkedIn in one sequence

Tantra receives

  • Sequence-linked WhatsApp replies in the unified inbox
  • Account sync and reconnect status

Requirements and permissions

  • An active Pro, Scale, or Enterprise plan

  • A WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app account on your phone

    The WhatsApp Business API and a separate business number are not required

  • Contact phone numbers in E.164 format with a country code

Scope and limits

  • Message steps are plain text.
  • Numbers need a country code in E.164 format.
  • A newly connected account may begin with gradual activity before ramping up.
  • Sending begins once the account finishes its initial sync.
  • Each connected WhatsApp account counts toward the social account allowance on your plan.
