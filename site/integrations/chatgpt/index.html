Integrations · AI personalization

Campaign personalization through your ChatGPT account.

Sign in with your own ChatGPT account, choose a model, test the personalization output, and set ChatGPT as your default provider. ChatGPT can also add prospects to your campaigns through Tantra's MCP server.

AvailableSign in with your account
Connect ChatGPTRead setup guide

Requires a ChatGPT account you can sign in to.

What you get

Sign in, no API key

Authenticate with your own ChatGPT account. Tantra stores the session encrypted and refreshes it for you.

Model selection

Choose from the models your signed-in account is entitled to use.

Personalization test

Run a full settings test and read the generated output before a campaign relies on it.

Active and default controls

Mark the provider active, and set it as Tantra's default personalization provider.

Template fallback

If the sign-in expires or the account hits its usage limit, the step sends your template instead of stalling.

Disconnect at any time

Remove the connection at any time; personalization then returns to the template.

Prospect intake over MCP

Connect ChatGPT to Tantra's MCP server and it can find, qualify and add prospects to the right omnichannel campaign, where they enter the sequence automatically.

Supported actions and data

Tantra can do

  • Send scrape content and recipient context to the model for personalization
  • Generate a personalized subject and body for email, or a description for a calendar invite
  • Add the prospects ChatGPT finds and qualifies to a campaign, through Tantra's MCP server

Tantra receives

  • The model's generated output, logged for cost and prompt tracking
  • The models your signed-in account is entitled to use
  • Prospects added to a campaign from ChatGPT

Requirements and permissions

  • A ChatGPT account you can sign in to

  • A Tantra account to store and test the connection

Scope and limits

  • Usage counts against your own ChatGPT subscription and its limits; Tantra does not mark up usage.
  • Setting this as your default provider is opt-in; Tantra never does it automatically.
  • Exactly one LLM provider is your default at a time.
  • With an expired sign-in or an account that has hit its usage limit, campaign personalization falls back to the plain template.
  • Campaign AI applies to email subject and body and to Calendar invitation descriptions. Social messages use templates, merge tags, and spintax.
  • Adding prospects over MCP uses a Tantra API key, not your ChatGPT sign-in. ChatGPT personalization and ChatGPT prospect intake are connected separately.
