WhatsApp as a Sequence Channel

Ordered Steps With Their Own Delays

Add a WhatsApp message at any point in the sequence, with its own delay. It follows the same rules as every other step: it runs in order, and the sequence stops for that contact when a reply is detected.

Phone Numbers Normalized on Import

Phone numbers are normalized on import, so lists with mixed formats still resolve. A contact without a usable number skips the WhatsApp step and remains in the sequence for the channels that can reach them.
