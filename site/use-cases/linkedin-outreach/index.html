Three Actions in a Defined Order

Visit, Connect, Then Message

A profile visit precedes the connection request, which can carry a short personalized note. A message step can wait until the request is accepted, so messages go only to accepted connections.

Per-Account Pacing and Limits

Actions on each account are spaced apart rather than sent in bursts, within that account's daily and weekly limits. Tantra reserves capacity before every action, so concurrent jobs cannot spend the same allowance twice.

Choose the Right LinkedIn Account

Your LinkedIn subscription sets how much outreach an account can safely carry. Sales Navigator gives the most room; free accounts are not built for outreach.

Free

Not suitable for outreach

$0

About 3 personalized connection notes a month (200 characters each), no InMail, and a commercial-use limit on search.

Premium

Works, with lower headroom

Career about $39.99/mo. Business about $59.99 to $69.99/mo.

InMail: 5 a month on Career, 15 on Business. Search returns up to 1,000 results per query.

Sales Navigator

Recommended

Core $119.99/mo, or $89.99/mo billed annually. Advanced $159.99/mo, or $149.99/mo billed annually.

50 InMails a month and up to 2,500 search results per query.

USD list prices, September 2026; vary by region and tax. Confirm the current price at checkout.
