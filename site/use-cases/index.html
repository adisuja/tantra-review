When You Need Google Workspace

Sequences combine Gmail, LinkedIn and WhatsApp steps, and calendar invitations currently run as their own campaign type. Workspace is needed only where Google sends the message.

Using Social Channels Responsibly

  • Connect only LinkedIn and WhatsApp accounts that you own or are authorized to use.
  • Follow each platform’s terms of use for messaging and automation.
  • Keep daily volumes conservative, especially on new or lightly used accounts.
  • Tantra’s default limits apply per account. You can lower them for any account, but not raise them.
