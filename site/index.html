    You'll need three things

    1. 1

      Google Workspace, authorized once

      Your Google admin

      Your admin grants Tantra access in the Google Admin console one time. After that, you verify each mailbox you send from.

      Setup guide
    2. 2

      Your LinkedIn and WhatsApp accounts

      You

      Sign in to LinkedIn, or scan a QR code with your existing WhatsApp number. Pro includes 5 accounts. For LinkedIn, Sales Navigator gives the most room.

      Account guide
    3. 3

      Your contact list

      You

      A CSV with emails, plus LinkedIn URLs or phone numbers. Upload up to 50,000 contacts at a time and map the columns in one screen.

      Import guide

    Add when you're ready

    From Connected Accounts to Booked Meetings

    Six steps from setup to reporting. Choose the campaign type first, and Tantra guides the accounts, audience, senders, timing and follow-up that path requires.

    See How It Works
    1

    Connect

    Connect the mailboxes, social accounts and providers your campaign uses.

    2

    Choose

    Choose a Calendar campaign or an omnichannel sequence, including a social-only sequence.

    3

    Build

    Add contacts by CSV, webhook, API or your AI assistant, order the steps, assign senders and add AI personalization where it improves the message.

    4

    Launch

    Confirm sender readiness, sending windows, account limits and stop conditions before launch.

    5

    Respond

    Handle replies in one inbox, with intent classified and the sequence stopped for that contact once the reply is detected.

    6

    Measure

    Report on RSVPs, replies and webinar attribution, and send events to your own systems by webhook.
