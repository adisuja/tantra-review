Use the unified inbox

Read and filter email, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp conversations in one inbox and view every conversation with the same person.

beginner·5 min read·Updated Sep 30, 2026

Open in Tantra

Inbox

These open the app in a new tab. You may be asked to sign in.

Overview#

The unified inbox brings campaign conversations from email, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp into one place. Each conversation keeps its channel, person, account, and campaign context visible so you can understand where the reply came from.

Opening a conversation marks it as read.

Before you start#

  • A campaign must have sent at least one message before its conversation can appear.
  • The sending account must remain connected so new messages can sync.
  • LinkedIn and WhatsApp conversations require an active plan and a connected account for that channel.

Find and open a conversation#

  1. Open Inbox.
  2. Use All or Unread to control which conversations appear.
  3. Select a channel filter: Email, LinkedIn, or WhatsApp.
  4. Use the campaign and sender filters when you want to focus on one campaign or account.
  5. Search by a person's name, address, handle, or message text.
  6. Select a conversation to open the full message history.

The channel icon beside a conversation tells you where it happened. The thread view shows the campaign and sending account connected to that conversation.

See every conversation with the same person#

When Tantra can match channel identities to the same contact record, the person panel shows their other conversations across channels, accounts, and campaigns. Select another conversation to move between them without searching again.

If two conversations are not grouped, confirm that both channel identities belong to the same contact record.

Understand email reply labels#

Incoming email can show classification and intent labels, such as reply, bounce, automatic response, unsubscribe, positive, negative, or neutral. These labels help you prioritize follow-up.

See Reply detection and intent classification and Reply intents explained.

Reply from the inbox#

Open the conversation and use the reply box when it is available for that channel. If Tantra shows a preview notice instead of sending, keep your draft and reply from the channel's normal app. The reply will appear in Tantra after conversations sync.

For email, keep your reply on the original thread so it can be matched to the campaign and stop later follow-ups correctly.

What happens next#

  • New messages appear after the connected account syncs. A short delay is normal.
  • Opening a conversation clears its unread state.
  • A genuine email reply stops that contact's remaining email follow-ups.
  • You can open the linked contact from the conversation context and update their lead stage.

Best practices#

  • Start with Unread, then filter by channel or campaign.
  • Keep every identity for one person on the same contact record.
  • Reconnect disconnected accounts promptly so conversation history stays current.
  • Review the channel icon before replying so you continue in the intended place.

Troubleshooting#

SymptomFix
A recent message is missingAllow a short time for the connected account to sync, then refresh
One channel has no conversationsConfirm your plan is active and the channel's account is Connected
Conversations for one person are separateLink the relevant identities to the same contact record
Sending from the reply box shows a preview noticeReply from the channel's normal app and keep the same email thread where applicable
The inbox is emptyConfirm that a campaign has sent and that its sending accounts remain connected

FAQ#

Which channels appear in the inbox?#

Email, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp.

Does opening a conversation mark it read?#

Yes.

Why do replies take time to appear?#

Connected accounts sync on a schedule, so a new message may not appear instantly.

Can I see one person's conversations across channels?#

Yes, when their channel identities are linked to the same contact record.
