01 · Connect

  • Google Workspace admin
  • Marketer or SDR

Connect the accounts you send from.

Your Workspace admin authorizes Tantra once for Gmail and calendar invitations. Each sender links their own LinkedIn or WhatsApp account. Add an AI or webinar provider only if a campaign uses one.

03 · Build

  • Marketer or SDR

Bring in your contacts and write the steps.

Import a CSV, or send prospects in by webhook, API or AI assistant. Organize contacts into lists, then write each step with merge tags, spintax or optional AI. Invalid and role addresses are held for review, not emailed.

Build a multichannel sequence
  1. 1

    Import or send in prospects

    Map CSV columns to contact fields and group contacts into lists, or let webhooks, the API or your AI assistant add prospects to a campaign automatically.

  2. 2

    Check email addresses

    Syntax, mail server and role-address checks, plus your suppression list, keep risky addresses out. Failures wait in Needs review.

  3. 3

    Write the campaign

    Create the invitation, or add sequence steps with a delay before each one.

  4. 4

    Personalize where useful

    Merge tags and spintax, or optional AI for email subject and body and calendar invitation text.

Social-only sequences need no Workspace

For example: profile visit → connection request → message after acceptance. Without a Gmail step, no Workspace mailbox is needed.

LinkedIn and WhatsApp messages use plain-text templates, merge tags and spintax. Optional AI applies to email and calendar invitation text only, and your stored copy is sent whenever personalization cannot run.

04 · Launch

  • Marketer or SDR

Launch with every account checked.

Before launch, Tantra checks your senders, contact list, plan and schedule. At each send it applies per-account limits and the sending window in the recipient's time zone.

Review and launch

The right sender for each channel

Gmail and calendar sends are spread across your verified Workspace mailboxes. LinkedIn and WhatsApp steps send from the account you select.

Timing and per-account limits

Mailbox daily and hourly caps are account-level settings, separate from each campaign’s schedule. LinkedIn and WhatsApp accounts carry their own limits.

Missing channel data skips a step

A missing email, LinkedIn profile or WhatsApp number skips that step while the sequence continues. A LinkedIn message after a connection request waits for acceptance.

06 · Measure & automate

  • RevOps or marketing lead

Report results by campaign type.

Sequences report replies, clicks and bounces. Calendar campaigns report RSVPs. Webinars report registrants and attributed attendees. Send results to the rest of your stack with webhooks, the API or MCP.

Understand your analytics

Sequence outcomes

Sent, derived delivered (sent minus bounced), replies, tracked clicks when enabled, bounces, and unsubscribes.

Calendar outcomes

Invitations sent and RSVP Yes, No or Maybe for each calendar campaign.

Webinar outcomes

Registrants, attendees, attributed attendees, and Zoom watch time where the provider returns it after a requested sync.

Link a synced webinar

Attach Zoom or WebinarGeek to the campaign.

Invite and attribute

When the campaign opts in, a Yes or Maybe registers the contact for the webinar.

Request attendee sync

Compare invitees with attributed attendance after refresh.

Zoom and WebinarGeek catalog, registrant, and attendance refreshes are user-triggered, not continuous background syncs. Sequence webinar links provide attribution only and do not register contacts.

Developer integrations

Webhooks

Send selected Tantra events to an HTTPS endpoint, and receive prospects into a campaign from your forms, CRM, Zapier, n8n or Make. Outbound delivery is at least once, so consumers deduplicate by event ID.

n8n, Zapier, and Make connect through the generic webhooks in both directions and are not native connectors. API-key scopes are not currently enforced, and MCP does not expose every action in the app.

Operational clarity

Know what runs automatically and what you refresh.

Automatic or scheduled

  • Scheduling and pacing for every active campaign
  • Sending, once each account passes its checks
  • Reply, bounce and unsubscribe handling
  • Calendar RSVP updates
  • Webhook delivery to your endpoints, with retries

User-triggered or configured

  • Connecting accounts and completing provider security checks
  • Zoom and WebinarGeek data refreshes
  • Launching or resuming a campaign
  • Choosing an AI default or tracking domain
