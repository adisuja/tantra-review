About Tantra

Outbound that runs on the accounts you already own

Tantra is an omnichannel outbound platform for B2B revenue teams. It coordinates cold email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp in one sequence, runs Google Calendar invitation campaigns, and brings every reply into one inbox.

Built by operators, not observers

Tantra comes from a team that has spent more than a decade building revenue engines for other companies. The platform turns that playbook into software.

What we believe

Your accounts, your reputation

Outreach should come from the mailboxes and profiles your buyers already recognize, never from a shared sending pool.

Signals that mean something

Tantra reports replies, clicks, bounces and RSVPs, and never uses open-tracking pixels.

One price per company

Published pricing with unlimited seats, email and calendar accounts, so growing the team never means renegotiating.

AI where it earns its place

AI personalization is optional and runs on your own provider, with your stored template as the fallback.
