Plans and Pricing

Pricing is per company, not per seat. Every plan includes unlimited email and calendar accounts. LinkedIn and WhatsApp accounts are included per plan: up to 5 on Pro and up to 15 on Scale.

Included Without Metering

Seats, contacts, mailboxes and campaigns are not billed by volume on any plan.

Sending mailboxes

Connect every Google Workspace mailbox your team uses. There is no per-mailbox charge.

Seats

Unlimited seats. Everyone who needs access can be invited, with no per-user charge and no seat count to manage.

Contacts

Unlimited contacts, with no list size tiers and no overage charges as lists grow.

Campaigns and sequences

Unlimited campaigns, sequences and steps, across both campaign types.

Calendar invitations

Calendar campaigns are not counted separately or billed per invitation.

AI inference

AI personalization is optional. Sign in with a ChatGPT or Claude account, or connect an OpenRouter, Cerebras or Novita key. Tantra adds no markup and sells no credits.

Both campaign types

Calendar invitation campaigns and sequences are included in every plan and are not sold separately.
